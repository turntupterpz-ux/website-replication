Before you can start purchasing postage, you’ll need to add a debit or credit card. There’s no monthly fees or costs; Pirate Ship is free, and you only pay the actual postage cost as you go!

Scales & Label Printers

You can measure your packages with any scale, as long as it gives you accurate weights in pounds and ounces.
You can print labels with any type of desktop printer, such as an inkjet or laser printer, or you can print with labels printers that support 2×7″ or 4×6″ labels.

Do you use a 2×7″ label printer?

If you use a Brother QL-800 series, Dymo 450, or other small label printer that prints 99019 labels, 2.4×7.5″ labels, or 2-5/16×7-1/2″ labels, change your Label Size to 2×7″ in Settings / General Settings
